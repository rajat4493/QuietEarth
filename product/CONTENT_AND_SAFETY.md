# Content, Sanskrit Sources, Safety

## Classical concepts allowed in MVP
Use carefully sourced passages/concepts such as:
- Bhagavad Gita 6.26: repeated return of the wandering mind.
- Bhagavad Gita 6.35: abhyasa + vairagya.
- Yoga Sutra 1.30–1.39: obstacles and multiple stabilizing approaches.
- Yoga Sutra 3.1–3.3: dharana, dhyana, samadhi distinction.
- Classical Yoga commentary: kshipta, mudha, vikshipta, ekagra, niruddha, clearly identified as commentarial rather than directly Patanjali’s sutra list.

## Copy rules
- Never call Sanskrit categories diagnoses.
- Never claim a practice treats a psychiatric disorder.
- Never say “scientifically proven for you” from in-app data.
- Never promise trance, enlightenment, hormone changes, trauma resolution, or disease benefit.

## Breathing safety
MVP uses only comfortable breathing cues. No forceful pranayama, prolonged retention, hyperventilation, or advanced breathwork.

## Crisis / medical boundary
This is a meditation-learning product, not crisis support or therapy. If future free-text analysis detects severe-risk language, do not build an amateur crisis classifier into MVP; instead keep the system out of diagnostic inference and provide static support information where policy/product review requires it.
