# QuietEarth visual completion

The visible identity is QuietEarth. Open supplied the reference for restrained composition; the landscape direction draws on the sense of place requested through Calm and Portal. The interface uses neutral grey, white and charcoal without ivory or teal.

The opening uses a bundled, still landscape. Reading screens remain plain with responsive system typography, a single initial hypothesis, optional evidence disclosures, and neutral disagreement styling. Questionnaire content scrolls at large text sizes. Continuous decorative animation is removed, and light/dark button foregrounds are paired for contrast. The privacy screen contains only available choices, without recommendation badges or future-milestone placeholders.

## Image provenance

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool; bundled at `ProjectStill/Assets.xcassets/QuietEarthLandscape.imageset/landscape.png`. The app icon is a square crop of the same asset.

Prompt: “Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a portrait photographic landscape asset for QuietEarth iPhone meditation app. Still, spacious, premium editorial photograph of layered slate-grey mountain ridges receding into soft silver mist under a vast pale cool grey sky. Bottom third dark textured rock ridge, middle distant hazy mountains, upper half empty sky. Subtle natural light, restrained almost monochrome palette, no teal, no ivory, no beige, no green cast, no sun disk. Fine real photographic detail, calm atmospheric depth. No people, buildings, text, logo, graphic overlays. Portrait 1024x1536.”

The asset is generated imagery, not a photograph of an identified real location. No network access is required to display it. This is an inclusive design pass, not clinical validation for ADHD or OCD.
