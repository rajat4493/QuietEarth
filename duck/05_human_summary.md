# TheDuck — Human Summary

We are building a small, serious meditation app that does not begin by telling everyone to breathe for ten minutes.

It first asks how the user's mind behaves, optionally lets the user bring in a privacy-preserving profile generated inside their own ChatGPT/Claude, and then shows where self-perception and observed behavior agree or disagree.

The app forms a provisional attention hypothesis, chooses a short 7-day practice experiment, explains why, and updates the model based on what actually happens.

Success for MVP is not “lots of meditations.” Success is that Client 0 can complete the full loop and feel that the app understood something real, challenged something questionable, prescribed something plausible, and learned from the result.

## Build status
M0 is complete. ProjectStill now opens as a native iPhone app with the Quiet Earth visual foundation and working routes from the opening screen to either a short explanation or the privacy-choice screen. The app clearly offers a questionnaire-only path before any conversation-derived evidence. No personal data is collected, stored, or sent in this milestone. Questionnaire behavior begins in M1 after milestone review.

M1 is complete. A user can now finish a 12–15 question adaptive intake and see a provisional profile made of individual attention dimensions, evidence statements, confidence, and visible contradictions. The app explains any broader interpretation instead of presenting it as a permanent type. Progress and completed profiles stay on the device and survive relaunch, and answers can be reviewed and changed. No AI import, external API, or meditation recommendation has been added.

M2 was built and is now in rework. A user could copy a constrained analysis prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or another assistant and paste back only the structured profile they approve. ProjectStill validates and previews that profile locally, marks every observation with its provider and confidence, and shows “How the two views compare.” Agreement, disagreement, alternatives, and source-specific scores remain visible. A realistic weak-self-reported-focus/strong-observed-persistence case produces “Capacity present, gating appears variable” rather than an averaged “medium focus.” AI evidence can be removed to restore the questionnaire-only result. The app still makes no external API call and provides no meditation recommendation.

M1.6 changes the method before we build any further, and it changes it for a good reason: a frontier model declined the analysis prompt we were shipping, and it was right to. We had asked it to rate ten named attention constructs from 0 to 1 with a confidence decimal each. Even with "do not diagnose" attached, that is the shape of a cognitive screening test — and worse, a number like "focus persistence 0.63" was never measured by anything. It was an impression printed to two decimal places, and we were then doing arithmetic on it.

So we are changing the product rather than the wording. External assistants are now asked to describe what they can actually observe in a conversation — "this person often opens related questions before finishing the first one" — with a plain strength rating of strong, moderate, weak, or insufficient, and no numbers at all. The app turns those descriptions into working hypotheses like "your attention may branch before it settles", states what a week of practice should look like if that is true and what would show it is false, and then lets the practice supply the evidence. The meditation becomes the measurement instead of the chat transcript. Questionnaire scoring stays inside the app to choose practices consistently, but it is never shown to the user as a score and never described as a measurement.

M1.6 also resets how the app looks. The earthy palette had drifted into beige on beige and read like a wellness brochure — calm had turned into tired. The new direction, Sunlit QuietEarth, is bright, spacious and optimistic: a warm ivory canvas, clean white cards, fresh sea-glass green with coral and lemon accents, deep spruce text, one confident hero visual per screen, and livelier motion. Gentler Streak is the quality bar we are measuring ourselves against; the identity stays ours through the flowing attention-path illustration, which now draws the method itself — two ribbons that touch where your self-description and the observations agree, and separate where they do not.

M1.6 is now built, not just written. The app asks for observations instead of scores, rejects a pasted result that contains any score-shaped number or diagnostic language, shows each observation next to a theme you confirm yourself, and turns what you confirm into hypotheses with a stated prediction and a stated way to be proven wrong. The profile screen no longer shows a single percentage anywhere; confidence is a word. Any AI evidence stored under the old format is deleted on first launch rather than converted, and the app says so.

One honest caveat: none of this has been compiled. The work was done in a Linux container with no Xcode, so the build and the 20-odd tests have been written but never run, and no screenshots exist under the new look. Per TheDuck that means M1.6 is incomplete until someone opens it on a Mac, runs the suite, and captures the evidence. Expect to fix compile errors on the first pass.
